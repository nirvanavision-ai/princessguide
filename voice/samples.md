# Writing Samples — Raw

**This is the highest-value file in the repo.** Everything else is you
*describing* your voice. This is you *using* it.

## What to paste

Anything you actually wrote, unedited:

- Texts and DMs, especially ones that made someone laugh
- Instagram captions
- The long group-chat message where you explained the plan for the night
- A voice note, transcribed (your phone will do it — voice notes are the closest
  thing to pure voice, because you don't self-edit out loud)
- An email where you were annoyed
- A review you left somewhere
- The note you typed at 4 a.m. that you've never shown anyone

**Do not clean any of it up.** Typos, lowercase, run-ons, the three periods you
use instead of commas, the way you spell "sooo" — that's the fingerprint. A
tidied sample teaches me to write tidy, which is exactly the problem.

Twenty short samples beat two long ones. Range matters more than length: I need
to hear you excited, you annoyed, you giving instructions, and you being funny.
