# Your Reference Universe

The single fastest way to stop sounding generic. Generic writing reaches for
the category — "a nightclub," "a good bag," "a great song." You reach for the
instance. Instances can't be guessed, only known.

Fill in what comes instantly. Skip what you'd have to think about. If a line
takes effort to answer, it isn't part of your world yet and it'll read as
research when it lands on the page.
