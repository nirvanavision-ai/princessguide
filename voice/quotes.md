# Your Quote Bank

Every chapter carries **your own lines** in two places:

- **The epigraph** — one line under the chapter title. Sets the temperature.
- **Pull quotes** — one or two mid-chapter. The lines readers screenshot.

These are yours to write. The skill will never fill them in with something it
made up — an empty slot gets a placeholder block asking for it, and that
placeholder stays in the manuscript until you replace it.

## What makes a good one

Short. Said out loud, not composed. A quote that sounds *written* dies on the
page; a quote that sounds like something you actually said to someone in a car
at 1 a.m. is the whole book in one line.

The test: could you say it to a friend without them noticing you'd rehearsed it?

Don't try to write all sixteen. Write the ones that arrive. Most of these will
come to you sideways, while you're doing something else — that's normal, and
it's why this file exists separately from the drafting.

## The things people say about you

Half-compliments, accusations, and the line a friend uses to describe you to
someone who hasn't met you. These make extraordinary epigraphs because they let
the reader see you from outside.
