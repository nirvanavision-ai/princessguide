## A: — the noise complaint

> "What are the books? Like, every day, Moira? Yeah. But it's acceptable to the
> house. I was flattered at first, so she texted and said, You're being too
> loud. Dude, I'm on FaceTime with family. Wow, I just went outside, and I
> didn't hear a thing. The voices in your head go, oh…"

<sub>Verbatim, transcription rough at the start. Reads as: a neighbour (Moira?)
texted to say she was being too loud, when in fact she was on FaceTime with
family — and when she stepped outside to check, she couldn't hear a thing.</sub>

---

**Why this earns a place:** Chapter 7 already carries neighbours and the noise
curfew as a hard constraint — *the after-party that ends in a complaint ends the
venue, and the venue is your home.* This is the funnier, truer version of that
rule, because the complaint wasn't even warranted. It lets the chapter make the
point without moralising: you manage the neighbour relationship not because
you're always in the wrong, but because being right doesn't get your building
back.

*"The voices in your head go, oh…"* is a great closing beat and should survive
into the prose more or less as-is.

**Open follow-ups:**
- Is Moira the neighbour, or the person you were on FaceTime with?
- *"I was flattered at first"* — flattered by what? This is the part I can't
  reconstruct and it sounds like the funniest beat in the story.
- Did you reply to her? What happened next?

## A: — the comedown

> "the Jay Z was such a peak moment. Like we went so high there at that concert,
> we felt so hard… the vibes were off… we got so high, you gotta come down. They
> are. Yeah. That's just fucked up."

**Why this belongs in Chapter 7:** the recovery section is currently written as
logistics — water, makeup off, shoes elevated. This is the emotional half nobody
in the genre writes down: the better the peak, the harder the landing, and the
comedown is a real event you can plan for rather than be ambushed by. *"You
gotta come down. That's just fucked up."* said flat is the whole idea.

**Open follow-ups:**
- What do you actually do the day after a peak like that?
- Was the comedown worse because the night was better?
