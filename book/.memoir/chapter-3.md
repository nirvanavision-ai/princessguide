**Why this is the best line in the transcript:** *in a moving vehicle while
wearing full glam* is exactly the specificity the book runs on. Nobody invents
that detail. It's funny without being told to be funny, and it's a real
operational lesson wearing a joke.

**The rule it proves:** the cabin is a controlled environment and nobody touches
your face in it. Chapter 3's seating doctrine already assigns seats before
departure — this extends it. Hands to yourself, help is offered and not
administered, and any repair that needs doing gets done stationary. It also
feeds the Communication Trio beat: the person who is a nuisance all night is a
logistics problem, and handling him is the handler's job, not yours.

**Cross-reference:** the eye/full-glam collision belongs in Chapter 6's beauty
emergencies too — a poked eye in full glam at the start of a night is a
ninety-second repair that most people would let ruin an hour.
