# Memoir Notes — Raw Author Material

One file per chapter: `chapter-1.md` … `chapter-7.md`.

These are the author's own words, captured verbatim during the interview gate
and **never rewritten in place**. The chapters quote from here; nothing quotes
back. Do not delete these files — regenerating a chapter reads them, and if
they're gone the anecdotes revert to empty placeholder blocks.

Format:

```markdown
# Memoir Notes — Chapter N: <Title>
> Raw author material. Quoted, never rewritten. Do not delete.

**Session:** <date>

## Q: <question as asked>
**Selected:** <option, if any>

## A:
<the author's words, verbatim>

---
**Used in:** <section name, once drafted>
```

Nothing in here is publication-ready and that's the point. Half-sentences,
names, prices, and the parts that reflect poorly on everyone involved are
exactly what the chapters need.
