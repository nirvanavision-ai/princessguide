## What the placeholders look like

Two kinds of blank get left in a drafted chapter, and both are meant to be
filled by the author, not by the skill:

**`> [!MEMOIR PROMPT]`** — a story slot. 150–250 words about something that
actually happened. Answer these out loud in the interview and they get written
for you; skip and the block stays until you come back.

**`> [!YOUR QUOTE]`** — an epigraph or pull quote. Your own line, in your own
words. The skill will never write one of these for you, not even as a draft to
replace, because a plausible fake in your voice is exactly the thing that ends
up in print by accident. Bank lines in [`../voice/quotes.md`](../voice/quotes.md)
as they come to you.
