# How to Party Like a Princess

### A Field Manual for the Structurally Glamorous

---

*For the girl standing on a curb at 1:40 a.m. doing math in a shoe she was
warned about.*

---

> **True princesses don't outsource logistics — they master them.**

That is the whole book. Everything else is implementation.

The world is full of people who think glamour is a mood you arrive in. It isn't.
It's a build. It has a schedule, a budget, a load-out, a comms plan, a
contingency for each of the six things that will actually go wrong, and an exit
strategy that was decided before anyone left the apartment. The women who make
it look effortless are running the most infrastructure in the room. That's the
trick. That's the only trick.

Anyone can be handed a good night. This is about producing one.

## How to Read This Book

Seven chapters, running in real time — from twenty-four hours out to the
morning after, in the order the night actually happens.

Every chapter ends two ways. First, **The Princess Protocol**: the rules,
stripped of commentary, formatted to be screenshotted and consulted in a
bathroom at 12:50 a.m. That page is the working document. Second, a sign-off,
which is the part you say to yourself in the mirror.

Read it front to back once. After that, it's a reference manual — and it is
meant to be used that way, which is to say opened mid-crisis with one hand.

## A Note on the Stories

The anecdotes in this book are the author's own. They were extracted under mild
duress and are printed largely as told, including the parts that reflect
poorly on everyone involved.

Where you see a **MEMOIR PROMPT** block, that story hasn't been written down
yet. Consider it an open seat at the table.
